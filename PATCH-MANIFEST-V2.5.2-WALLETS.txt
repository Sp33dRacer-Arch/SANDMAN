SANDMAN V2.5.2 PayPal Wallets Patch

Purpose:
- Remove Stripe from the customer-facing website checkout and admin payment UI.
- Keep PayPal as the primary checkout provider.
- Add Google Pay and Apple Pay through PayPal's wallet integrations, gated by real browser/account eligibility.
- Pause new marketplace checkout until a PayPal-compatible multiparty seller-payout flow is implemented.

This patch intentionally retains legacy server-side Stripe-related database/service compatibility for historical orders and records. It does not expose Stripe as a new website checkout option.
