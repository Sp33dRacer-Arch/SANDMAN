WHAT V1.4.2 FIXED
- Accepted-offer pricing is limited to exactly one unit at cart, checkout and defensive price-calculation layers.
- Mixed-order fulfillment only counts shipped supplier fulfillments that still have active, non-fully-refunded dropship lines.
- Supplier inventory is reserved atomically at checkout with reservedStock + availableStock.
- Checkout rollback, cancellation and expiry release uncommitted supplier reservations.
- Fully-refunded dropship lines release uncommitted supplier reservations.
- Supplier feeds preserve live reservations when reported stock changes.
- Releasing a supplier hold recalculates availability from the latest supplier-reported stock, preventing a feed change from recreating sold-out inventory.
- Supplier submission commits the local hold only after the external supplier order has been persisted, with a retry/repair path for interrupted local commits.
- Public product/search/recommendation availability uses availableStock rather than raw supplier stock.
