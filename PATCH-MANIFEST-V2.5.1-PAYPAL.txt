SANDMAN V2.5.1 PayPal-first hardening patch
Base: SANDMAN V2.5.0 (commit 93e3d65)

Purpose
- Make PayPal the default/primary ordinary checkout provider.
- Harden PayPal Orders v2 create/capture/refund flows.
- Add signed raw-body PayPal webhook verification and event deduplication.
- Persist PayPal capture IDs for reliable refunds.
- Send merchant-provided physical shipping addresses to PayPal.
- Add PayPal-specific launch-readiness checks and storefront/admin messaging.
- Preserve Stripe as an optional fallback/current marketplace path; this patch does not claim PayPal multiparty marketplace payouts.
