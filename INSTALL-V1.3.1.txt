V1.3.1 SAFETY CHANGES
=====================
- Marketplace stock is atomically reserved during checkout.
- Failed payment initialization restores seller stock and preserves the cart.
- Stripe webhook success checks PaymentIntent id, amount, currency and provider.
- PayPal capture checks local order identity, invoice, amount and currency.
- Seller payouts are created after payment but only released after all of that seller's items in the order are marked shipped.
- Stripe Connect transfers use idempotency keys and support retry.
- Supplier fulfillment is unique per order/supplier and failed submission can be retried.
- Mixed dropship + marketplace fulfillment status is recomputed centrally.
- Access JWTs are memory-only in the web UIs.
- Persistent login uses the Secure/HttpOnly refresh cookie.
- Access JWTs are bound to a server-side session, so logout/password change revokes them.
- Admin cancellation of unpaid orders restores reserved marketplace stock.

IMPORTANT
=========
Do not store raw card or bank account numbers in SANDMAN. Owner payouts and seller payout
destinations are configured in Stripe/PayPal secure hosted interfaces.
