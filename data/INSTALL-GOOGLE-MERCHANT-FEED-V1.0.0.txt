SANDMAN GOOGLE MERCHANT FEED V1.0.0 — INSTALL

What this adds:
A new route, src/modules/feeds/feeds.routes.ts, that serves your live
product catalog as a Google Merchant Center feed at
/feeds/google-merchant.txt. It reads your real prisma/schema.prisma to map
your actual field names (title, price, stock, images, brand, gtin, styles),
reuses your existing prisma client and asyncHandler helper instead of
creating new ones, and tries to wire itself into your app next to your
existing experienceRouter.

It never touches Prisma schema/migrations, .env, secrets, payment settings,
or supplier-order settings, and it never overwrites an existing
feeds.routes.ts unless you pass -Force.

1. Extract this ZIP outside the backend folder.
2. Copy the extracted files into SANDMAN-backend without replacing .env,
   .git or node_modules.
3. From the backend root run:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\APPLY-GOOGLE-MERCHANT-FEED-V1.0.0.ps1

4. Read the console output carefully. It tells you:
   - exactly which Product (and, if present, variant/style) fields it
     detected and used
   - which style/variant mode it picked and why
   - whether it could auto-wire the route into your app, or whether you
     need to add two lines yourself
5. Do not commit or deploy unless the final line is:
SANDMAN Google Merchant feed V1.0.0 install checks passed.
6. Open src/modules/feeds/feeds.routes.ts and set the two TODOs near the
   top: CURRENCY and SITE_URL.
7. Start your dev server and open http://localhost:<port>/feeds/google-merchant.txt.
   Check a few real rows by eye — title, price, image_link and availability
   should all look right.
8. Only once that looks right, register the URL in Google Merchant Center
   as a Scheduled fetch source (see the step-by-step Claude gave you in
   chat), and deploy so the URL is publicly reachable over HTTPS.

If a field mapping looks wrong (e.g. picked the wrong price field, or
google_product_category is blank because your schema has no matching
field), open feeds.routes.ts directly and adjust it by hand — it's a plain
readable file, not something you need to re-run the installer for.

To regenerate after a schema change:
node .\scripts\apply-google-merchant-feed-v1.0.0.mjs --force
(or run the .ps1 with -Force)
